# Play Store listing — English

**Title** (30 chars max): `Shopware Shop Manager`

**Short description** (80 chars max):
> Manage your Shopware 6 shop on the go: orders, reviews, promotions, stock.

**Full description:**

Shopware Shop Manager is a companion app for merchants running Shopware 6. Connect
your shop with your admin login and handle day-to-day operations from your phone —
no desktop needed.

**Dashboard** — today's revenue against your daily target, order counts, a 7-day
revenue chart and a "needs attention" feed: pending reviews, overdue payments and
products running low on stock.

**Orders** — browse, search and filter your orders (status, payment, delivery, date,
value, payment method, sales channel, documents). Open an order to see line items,
totals and addresses, change order/payment/delivery state, edit tracking codes,
add internal notes and generate or share invoice and delivery-note PDFs.

**Reviews** — approve or reject product reviews from a mobile inbox.

**Promotions** — see active, scheduled and ended promotions, toggle them and
generate individual codes.

**Products** — fix stock, price and visibility right from the low-stock list, and
shoot a new product photo with your camera.

**Customers** — searchable customer list with order history and contact actions.

**Built for real operations:**
- Multiple shops, each with its own content language and color tag
- Background sync with notifications for new orders and reviews (low-stock and
  overdue-payment alerts are opt-in)
- English and German, phone and tablet layouts
- Works with self-hosted instances (including plain-HTTP on your LAN)

**Privacy by design:** the app talks only to your own shop's Admin API. Your
password is used once to sign in — only a revocable session token is stored,
encrypted with hardware-backed keys. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party
servers.

Requires a Shopware 6 shop (6.5 or newer recommended; verified against 6.7) and an
admin user account. This app is an independent community project and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by shopware AG.
